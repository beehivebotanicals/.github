## Welcome to Beehive Botanicals  

We are a socially responsible, cruelty free and environmentally friendly manufacturer in Hayward, Wisconsin and a leader in the natural products industry. We have been dedicated to purity, integrity and quality since 1972. People are choosing nature-based bee products, and for a good reason, to support the world as a whole, but also to build healthy bodies the way nature intended.
